Lesson 7: Embarrassing moments

The main pronouns are:
she          he          it          we          they          them

Pronouns like she, he, it, we, they, them and the word there are very useful words.
We can use them instead of repeating nouns.

Examples:

My father went to the shop on the corner. He bought a magazine there.
The cat ran up the tree. It got stuck there.

The boys played in the park. They loved it there.

My sister and | are visiting our grandparents next week.
We can’t wait to see them.

Sentences with pronouns and there come after sentences with nouns.
